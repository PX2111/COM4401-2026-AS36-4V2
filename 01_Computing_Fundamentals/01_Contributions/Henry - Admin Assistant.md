### Component Justifications

#### Case
* **Model:** NZXT H5 Flow Micro ATX Mid Tower Black (with Tinted Tempered Glass Side Panel and USB 3.2 Gen 2x2 Type-C, USB 3.2 Gen 1 Type-A Front Panel)
* **Justification:** I chose this case because it is affordable and highly compatible with all the selected components. I explicitly avoided expensive aesthetic features like built-in RGB lighting, as they do not offer functional value to justify an extra £200 cost.
---

#### CPU
* **Model:** AMD Ryzen 3 3200G
* **Justification:** This processor is ideal for low-cost office and administrative systems, which do not require the heavy computing or graphical performance of high-end CPUs. I chose the "G" version to utilize its integrated graphics, which are highly rated for low-to-mid-range setups. This allows the system to support multiple displays without a dedicated graphics card. Additionally, its expected lifespan of 8-10 years provides sufficient future-proofing for office work.
---

#### Motherboard
* **Model:** Asus TUF GAMING A520M-PLUS WIFI AM4 DDR4 Micro ATX
* **Justification:** This motherboard provides all necessary features for an administrative assistant at a budget-friendly price point. It natively supports the chosen CPU, includes a built-in Network Interface Card (NIC) for connectivity, and offers 4 RAM slots alongside 3 PCIe expansion slots for future flexibility.
---

#### RAM
* **Model:** Patriot Signature Line SODIMM DDR4-2400 CL19 12GB (3 x 4GB)
* **Justification:** I selected three 4GB sticks of DDR4 RAM to reach a total of 12GB. At only £20 per stick, this configuration is highly economical. A 12GB capacity is more than enough for daily administrative duties, allowing multiple standard office programs to run simultaneously without bottlenecking performance or crossing the 10GB threshold.
---

#### CPU Cooler
* **Model:** AMD Wraith Stealth Socket AM4
* **Justification:** I opted for this highly reviewed budget CPU fan to prevent the processor from overheating. It is inexpensive, reliable, and user reviews frequently praise its exceptionally quiet operation. it would be perfect for the job role as the workload for the cpu won't be too bad even with multitasking.
---

#### Storage
* **Model:** Kingston NV3 500GB SSD M.2-2280 PCIe 4.0 X4 NVMe
* **Justification:** This 500GB m.2-2280 NVMe SSD offers high speeds and an excellent storage-per-cost ratio. Because modern enterprise businesses heavily rely on cloud storage rather than fragmenting data across local machines, a fast 500TB drive provides more than enough capacity for system files, local caching and any other temporary files.
---

#### Power Supply (PSU)
* **Model:** Montech CENTURY II 850W 80+ Gold Certified Fully Modular
* **Justification:** This 850W power supply delivers more than enough wattage to run the entire system effortlessly and quietly. Its certified for 80+ Gold efficiency rating guarantees minimal energy waste, supporting an eco-friendly office environment and reducing long-term power usage. This power supply should be more than enough for the computer to run silently, witch is important when working in an office environment.
---
#### Case Fan
* **Model:** ARCTIC P12 120mm Black
* **Justification:** I added this basic 120mm fan to ensure steady, reliable airflow throughout the case and prevent any internal heat buildup. It is highly popular among PC builders for its airflow-to-cost ratio and only added a minor £3.99 to the overall budget. I thought that adding a casefan would improve device safety at a low cost and ensure all the components keep cool whist the admin is utilizing programs that are more heavy on CPU usage (eg. Microsoft Excel).
---
# Ethics 
in terms of the ethical considerations made, when selecting parts for admin assistant computers, I aimed to meet the role’s day-to-day needs without over-buying expensive, high-performance hardware that would go unused. I chose reliable and appropriate parts that support the job roles needs, while considering energy efficiency (especially in terms of power supply), durability, future-proofing and responsible purchasing from trusted vendors to reduce long-term costs and waste. Because an architecture firm handles confidential client information, building plans, budgets, and project documents, I also considered how the computers would protect sensitive data. Suitable devices, secure storage can be handled by encrypting drives, and role-based access help ensure staff can access the information needed for their work while limiting exposure of files they do not need.
---
### Conclusion
In conclusion, this hardware selection outlines a VERY highly cost-effective and reliable system tailored specifically to the demands of administrative and office work. By prioritizing utility over aesthetics-such as opting for a clean, its budget fits within the required £950 budget whist ensuring quality and futureproof components that should make Northwest Architecture Studio Ltd satisfied. Im justifying my saving of so much of the cost for all three systems by pointing out that for most administrative work, a cost effective solution is usually more worth it since replacing expensive parts every 7-10 years is extremly costly, and only for a slight boost in performance, basically going cheaper means that futureproofing is actually easier because buying cheap parts is much more cost effective.
